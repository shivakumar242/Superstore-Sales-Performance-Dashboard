# Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution developed to analyze sales, profit, product performance, and regional business trends.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key business KPIs, identify high-performing product categories, evaluate regional sales distribution, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Modeling
- Business Intelligence
- Data Visualization

---

## Business Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to:

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Track profitability across products and regions
- Analyze customer purchasing patterns
- Identify top-performing product categories
- Support strategic business decisions through visual analytics

## Dashboard Components

### 1. Sales by Sub-Category

Analyzes revenue generated by different product sub-categories.

Key Insights:
- Phones generated the highest sales.
- Storage and Tables were among the top-performing categories.
- Labels and Supplies contributed the lowest sales.

---

### 2. Sales by Region

Displays regional sales distribution using a donut chart.

Key Insights:
- West region contributed the highest share of sales.
- East region followed closely behind.
- South region generated the lowest revenue.

---

### 3. Monthly Sales Trend

Tracks sales performance throughout the year.

Key Insights:
- Sales increased significantly toward the end of the year.
- November recorded the highest sales.
- Consistent growth observed in Q4.

---

### 4. Quantity Sold by Region

Measures product quantity sold across regions.

Key Insights:
- West region sold the highest quantity.
- South region recorded the lowest volume.

---

### 5. Sales by State

Analyzes state-level sales performance.

Key Insights:
- Washington generated the highest sales.
- Virginia and Wisconsin also performed strongly.
- Several states contributed minimal revenue.

---

## Business Insights

- The West region is the strongest performing market.
- Phones are the most profitable product category.
- Sales show strong seasonal growth in the final quarter.
- Certain states contribute significantly more revenue than others.
- Product and regional analysis can help optimize inventory and marketing strategies.
